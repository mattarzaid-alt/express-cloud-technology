The front desk, on WhatsApp.

  • Speaks like Doha

    Natural Gulf Arabic, English, and Arabic typed in Latin letters. Never stiff textbook Arabic.

  • Books and reschedules

    Offers real open slots, confirms only once the booking is saved, and moves or cancels on request.

  • Answers the routine

    Prices, hours, directions, parking, payment and which doctor does what, from your own information.

  • Hands off to your team

    Medical questions, complaints and anything unusual go to your staff with a one-line summary.

It knows what it must never say.

  • No medical advice

    It never assesses symptoms or names a medication. It offers the earliest appointment and alerts your team.

  • Emergencies first

    Signs of an emergency get one answer straight away: call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

  • No invented answers

    If a price or policy isn't in your sheet, it checks with your team instead of guessing.

  • Minimal data

    It asks only for a name and a time. No ID numbers, insurance details or medical history over WhatsApp.
