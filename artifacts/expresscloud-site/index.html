Who we are

Qatar's technology investment partner since 2013.

Express Cloud Technology was founded in Doha with a clear mandate: to accelerate the growth of technology-driven businesses reshaping how people live, move, transact, and connect across Qatar and the wider region.

We go beyond capital. Our team brings hands-on operating experience in cloud infrastructure, digital platforms, and enterprise technology, giving our portfolio companies a genuine edge in building and scaling. We back founders who have the vision to lead, and we stay with them for the long run.

From early-stage fintech startups to growth-stage digital commerce platforms, Express Cloud Technology is the partner that understands both the technology and the market.

Where we invest

Four sectors. One mission.

  • Fintech

    From digital wallets to lending platforms, we back companies rebuilding how Qatar and the region's businesses and consumers manage, move, and grow their money.

  • Mobility

    We invest in the companies reimagining how goods and people move: last-mile logistics, fleet management, and smart urban transport solutions built for Gulf cities.

  • PropTech

    Qatar's real estate sector is evolving fast. We partner with companies bringing data, automation, and digital transactions to one of the region's most active property markets.

  • Digital commerce

    We support platforms and infrastructure enabling the next wave of e-commerce across Qatar, from B2C marketplaces to B2B procurement tools and cross-border trade technology.

The opportunity

Why Qatar and emerging markets?

Qatar is one of the world's fastest-digitalizing economies: high smartphone penetration, a young tech-savvy population, and government programs actively accelerating the shift. Express Cloud Technology is uniquely positioned to capture this momentum.

  • Qatar National Vision 2030 prioritizing a knowledge-based, diversified digital economy.
  • High internet adoption and mobile-first consumer behaviour across the Gulf.
  • Rapidly growing startup and SME ecosystem with significant unmet demand for tech infrastructure and capital.
Qatar is not catching up to the digital economy. It is building one of its own. Express Cloud Technology exists to make sure the right companies lead that charge.
